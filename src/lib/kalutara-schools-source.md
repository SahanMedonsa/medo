# Kalutara school picker data

415 government schools in Kalutara district (395 provincial, 20 national), filtered by district 13.Kalutara from Ministry of Education 2024 spreadsheets, retrieved 2026-10-01.

- https://dmb.moe.gov.lk/showStat2024.php
- https://dmb.moe.gov.lk/2024/01/01_Excel/Tbl20240102.xlsx
- https://dmb.moe.gov.lk/2024/01/01_Excel/Tbl20240101.xlsx

Names and census identifiers retain the source spelling. This is not a complete list of private or international schools or subsequent openings. The picker accepts custom school names and includes previously saved names.
